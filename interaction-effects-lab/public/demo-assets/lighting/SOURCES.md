# Lighting assets

The six morning and night WebP files are Viktor Oddy's original Lumen Roof sky images, full ground images and transparent house cutouts. They are the assets supplied by the author for the linked MotionSites template. [MotionSites template](https://motionsites.ai/?prompt=lumen-roof-hero) · [Author's demonstration](https://x.com/viktoroddy/status/2117456425245200595)

The lighting product version reuses the lab's room photograph from the VirtualFit case and the Siena chair image from the Forma Living case. The three scenes change the lighting treatment of those same images.
