# Orbit assets

- `phoenix.glb`, `kraken.glb`, `phoenix.webp`, and `kraken.webp` are the model and poster artwork supplied by Viktor Oddy and MotionSites for [Myth Atlas](https://motionsites.ai/?prompt=phoenix-ignis). The demo keeps the original textures and geometry appearance at reduced resolution and polygon count. [Original viewer](https://featured-creatures.vercel.app/)
- `chair.glb` is an optimized copy of the Sheen Chair by Eric Chadwick for Wayfair, LLC (2020), released under CC0 1.0. The six Forma Living previews use it as a base, with illustrative proportions and upholstery colors. [Model and license](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/SheenChair)
