Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: one fictional short-film atmosphere/storyboard board for a Chinese creative development dossier. Make ONE wide landscape triptych with THREE equally sized upright photographic panels, thin plain white gutters, overall approximately 9:4 aspect ratio, each panel approximately 3:4. Each panel must be a distinct readable moment, not a blended collage. No text, lettering, captions, logos or watermark. This is a constructed visual concept, not a real event. Ordinary phone/action-camera realism: believable imperfect framing, small-sensor texture, mild compression, natural exposure, coherent physical space and people, real skin and fabric. No glossy advertising retouch, no teal-orange movie grading, no artificial bokeh, no fantasy distortions. All people are adults. Keep the same people, clothes and props across panels. Photographs should make the viewpoint and emotional relationship immediately understandable. Story: a delivery rider unexpectedly finds that a romantic birthday dinner is waiting for him. STRICT first-person chest-camera view, camera belongs to a 30-year-old rider; only gloved or recently ungloved hands and rain-jacket edges visible, never a third-person view of him. Left: rider looking down a narrow damp stone service stair toward a natural limestone sea cave, cake box carried at lower edge, glimpses of blue sea and warm table light beyond; hose and utilitarian doorway make the route ordinary and real. Middle: same chest-camera arrives in a beautiful but realistically sized natural seaside cave restaurant, stone terrace solidly supported by rock with low railing, waves OUTSIDE the cave below, one small dinner table with two seats. An attractive 30-year-old woman with shoulder-length dark hair in a simple burgundy evening dress stands from the table and approaches with familiar warmth, not posed model glamour. Right: same woman close to the chest camera, smiling gently and reaching toward the rider's helmet strap at the upper edge while her other hand gestures to the empty chair; cake now on table with a few small lit birthday candles, two place settings. No text or names on cake. Eyes communicate 'I was waiting for you'. Adults, restrained natural intimacy. Cool sea light plus local warm lamps, textured cave and damp jacket, realistic restaurant equipment and railings. Emotional sequence: mundane task, extraordinary hidden place, being personally welcomed. Do not turn the cave into an underwater glass room or palace; this cave is above sea level.
