Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: one fictional short-film atmosphere/storyboard board for a Chinese creative development dossier. Make ONE wide landscape triptych with THREE equally sized upright photographic panels, thin plain white gutters, overall approximately 9:4 aspect ratio, each panel approximately 3:4. Each panel must be a distinct readable moment, not a blended collage. No text, lettering, captions, logos or watermark. This is a constructed visual concept, not a real event. Ordinary phone/action-camera realism: believable imperfect framing, small-sensor texture, mild compression, natural exposure, coherent physical space and people, real skin and fabric. No glossy advertising retouch, no teal-orange movie grading, no artificial bokeh, no fantasy distortions. All people are adults. Keep the same people, clothes and props across panels. Photographs should make the viewpoint and emotional relationship immediately understandable. Story: fleeting access to an enviably free mountain life. STRICT camera position throughout: a passenger with a phone INSIDE a descending enclosed gondola, slight reflections and a window edge visible, looking down diagonally at a believable snowy slope and a timber ski-in ski-out chalet beside it. Left: wide view from gondola, chalet terrace with breakfast table and coffee, a charismatic 30-year-old woman in a fitted but functional dark-red ski jacket and trousers, black snowboard boots, dark hair and goggles pushed onto helmet. An adult companion in a bathrobe stands behind the breakfast table. Chalet and terrace join a gentle groomed slope directly, no cliff jump. Middle: closer phone zoom through the same glass, the same woman looks up toward the passing gondola and lifts a coffee cup with a small confident smile; snowboard rests on snow beside terrace. Natural morning light, steam above cup, real boot tracks. Right: camera has turned to follow her downhill as the gondola also descends more slowly: same red-clad helmeted woman carving smoothly on a snowboard on the continuous slope, chalet receding behind, snow spray LOW at her board, not up at the gondola. She is skilled and relaxed. Emotional sequence: glimpse of private comfort, brief personal recognition, graceful freedom. Keep slope/gondola/chalet geography possible. No floating chalet, no aerial drone view, no hovering camera outside the gondola.
