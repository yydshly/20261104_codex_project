Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Create ONE original poetic photographic artwork, landscape 3:2, with convincing photographic materials and an extraordinary but immediately readable visual relationship. No text.

Reference images 1 and 2 are STYLE AND TECHNIQUE references ONLY: concentrated luminosity against quiet darkness, a readable dark living silhouette, fine wet surface detail, selective rim light and restrained environmental detail. Do not reuse their subjects, arrangement, circular geometry, vertical central axis or story. The new scene below must determine all objects, action, framing and spatial relationships.

New independent scene:
A solitary heron flies very low from left to right across a dark lake during an evening storm. Photograph it obliquely from the side, at water level. The long body and neck form a lean horizontal dark silhouette toward the left half. Only one nearer wing is seen sweeping down obliquely; the far wing is partly hidden and receding, never a symmetrical spread. The very last outer feather tip is brushing the water. Correct bird anatomy, taut but living feather structure, no fish or hunting action.

The lake holds the sharply luminous, angular reflection of a lightning flash that occurred in the sky OUTSIDE the crop. We see the reflected light only, not the actual sky bolt. This single irregular silver-white zigzag runs diagonally across the right half of the dark water. At the point where the heron's down-swept wingtip grazes the surface, a small real ripple bends and briefly separates a section of that reflected zigzag. A few tiny drops lift beside the feather tip and catch its pale light. The relationship should read at a glance: a fragile moving dark wing seems to interrupt something immense and untouchable. Let the visual coincidence carry the image; no caption or symbol is needed.

This is an improbable poetic photographic instant, not a demand for mundane documentary subject matter. Make the light, bird, water and reflection optically coherent, with believable wet materials. The reflected lightning is light on moving water: partly broken into thin uneven bands by low ripples, never electricity physically crawling on the lake, never a neon ribbon or drawn line. Keep most of its branching absent so there is one strong legible light shape, not a web. Some of the reflection may pass outside the lower-right crop. Do not frame or surround the bird with it.

Composition:
Wide, asymmetric lateral movement. Bird on the left, angular reflected light on the right and below, one small point of interaction between them. Broad quiet storm-dark negative space above and in front of the bird. The lake plane occupies most of the image; the horizon is extremely low-contrast and unobtrusive, with no land details or buildings. Do not put a bright object above an animal, do not cup, embrace or support a light source. No circular or crescent-shaped light.

Light and detail:
Deep slate-blue, charcoal and natural storm-gray water with a concentrated pearl-white reflected flash. Warm color is not required. The flash produces a fine pale rim on the wet wing edge and just enough light to reveal the head, bill and shoulder in shadow; the bird remains primarily a clean dark form. Fine feathers and a few microdroplets are sharp near the interaction, with softer distant water. Preserve the brightest reflection's irregular water texture and open shadow separations. Rich but restrained tonal depth, no heavy HDR halos, plastic feathers or equally bright texture everywhere. No gold water curtain, decorative glow, fantasy particles or dramatic volumetric rays.
