Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset: one original fine-art photographic image, portrait 4:5, no writing. No input images.
Creative direction: a red rain boot steps into a puddle and breaks a warm window reflection into small pieces of light. Priorities: warm color first, rich tactile detail and HDR-like tonal depth, then an immediately felt playful energy.

An extremely low, water-level close photograph on a quiet rain-wet brick lane at evening. Only one lower leg in plain dark navy trousers and its deep cherry-red rubber rain boot enter from the upper-left edge. The boot moves diagonally toward the lower-right. Catch the physically specific instant when the front sole has just pressed into shallow water and the heel is still slightly raised. Real weight and contact, an ordinary spontaneous playful step rather than a product pose. No face or upper body.

A large amber-lit window is reflected in the puddle across the middle-right and foreground. Enough of the far reflection remains calm and recognizably rectangular, with one or two subtle dark mullion lines. Near the boot, the step suddenly breaks that same window image: the displaced surface fans forward and to the sides in an irregular low transparent sheet, separating into several sharp droplets and small glinting fragments of the reflection. Let the eye see both the intact warm window-image and its lively disruption in a single glance. The reflection and water impact share the focus; do not make this only a shoe photograph.

Transparent, colorless water illuminated by a honey-gold light source outside the frame. Only some droplet edges and water-sheet rims shine amber; darker portions remain visibly transparent and reflect deep brown surroundings. A modest, physically credible splash for shallow water, uneven and directional, not a huge perfect crown, halo, circular curtain or decorative explosion. Larger droplets near the impact can be exquisitely sharp, with smaller ones falling naturally out of the focus plane. No suspended sparkles or particles.

A dramatic but clean warm palette: cherry and burgundy rubber, honey and amber reflection, burnt-umber wet bricks, near-black warm shadows. Keep broad dark areas around the concentrated window light. The actual source window can remain outside the frame; at the distant upper edge allow only a very restrained soft warm glow, never a row of bokeh balls or a busy street scene. No sky, horizon or skyline.

Tactile details: water beads and slight lived-in scuffs on curved red rubber, a small readable tread edge at the contact point, transparent tension in the thin splashing water, fine ripple ridges stretching the reflected mullions, quiet dark brick grain and narrow mortar joints. Wetness produces selective highlights, not glitter across every surface. The puddle must look clear, not muddy, greasy or metallic.

Fine-art high-speed photography with sophisticated HDR-style tonal treatment: retain individual detail inside bright water edges and warm reflection, retain rich shadow separations, strong dimensionality and luminous warm midtones. Crisp critical-plane detail, a soft natural background, restrained fine grain. Richly graded and visually arresting, without crunchy sharpening, HDR halos, plastic rubber, excessive saturation or muddy gray texture. Capture the pleasure of breaking a still pool of light through the visible event itself.

One complete photograph. No text, caption, brand marks, frame, collage, other props, leaves, toys, coins, gold dust, actual fire or molten metal. No animals, celestial discs, marine scene, stairs or cloud reflection. Do not imitate any existing image composition.
